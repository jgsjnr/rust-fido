🛡️ freertos_route.rs & Authorization Barrier
crates/aegis-fido-firmware/src/freertos_route.rs
Zero-Trust Barrier
Central CTAPHID and vendor opcode dispatcher. Implements the Zero-Trust Authorization Barrier.

• Standard FIDO2 (0x01..0x0B): MakeCredential, GetAssertion, GetInfo are Exempt from Secure Guard (Fail-Open for WebAuthn standard compliance).
• Vendor Flow (0x20..0x39): Keys, OATH, Wallets, PIV, Logs are Intercepted by authorize_vendor_opcode. Blocked unless Secure Guard is Unlocked.
Standard Flow 0x01..0x0B (Fail-Open WebAuthn)
Protected Flow 0x20..0x39 (Intercepted by Barrier)
SG Protocol 0x3A..0x3E (Wire Administration)
freertos_route.rs Zero-Trust Fail-Open WebAuthn Vendor Gate

🛡️ SecureGuardEngine (Zero-Trust)
crates/aegis-fido-core/src/secure_guard
10 States (0x00..0x09)
Pure #![no_std] zero-trust engine with 10 discrete states. Uses PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (10,000 iterations) with constant-time equality check (constant_time_eq_32). Caps consecutive PIN failures at 5 attempts, enforcing progressive backoff penalties and selective factory wipes.
States Managed Unprovisioned, Provisioning, Locked, Authenticating, Unlocked, RecoveryRequired, RateLimited, WipePending, WipeInProgress, ErrorLocked
Crypto Invariant PBKDF2-SHA256 + constant_time_eq_32
Brute-Force Guard 5 Attempts -> Progressive Backoff -> Wipe
no_std 10 States PBKDF2 Timing Attack Safe
